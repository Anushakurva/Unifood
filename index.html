Our Story

One Kitchen, Four Cuisines, One Family

Unifood began with a simple idea: Mahbubnagar shouldn't have to choose between a slow-cooked Hyderabadi biryani, a smoky Arabian mandi, a rich Moghlai curry, and a fiery wok of Chinese Manchurian.

Today we're a full multi-cuisine restaurant with open-air garden seating, private bamboo cabins, and a banquet hall for the town's celebrations.
